# Baku Metro Sign Drawings — Review Set 2 (Critical & Major Items)

**Mode:** document-review (skill `metro-wayfinding-audit`) · **Date:** 2026-11-16
**Files:** DC-S1-1.pdf, ID-W-S.pdf, ID-W-M.pdf, ID-W-L.pdf, ID-EXT-PM_3.pdf (identical to the earlier upload; earlier findings still apply)
**Scope:** only Critical, High and major-Medium items; minor editorial points left out.

> This is a review of design-intent drawings, not a station audit, and gives no compliance certification. Every item cites a drawing sheet. "Conflict" means two sheets give different values.

## A. Critical: answer before any production

| # | Sign | Issue | Evidence | Why it matters | Question / correction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | DC-S1-1 | Height is **350 mm** in the title block ("1250x350 1-sided") but **250 mm** (254) in the front view | DC-S1-1 p1 | The panel, face, LED layout, weight and fixing all depend on it; the supplier will build whichever value they read | Which is correct, 250 or 350? Reissue the sheet with one value. |
| C2 | DC-S1-1 | Underside **2100 mm** from floor; the other overhead signs (SN-C-L-2, SN-S-XL-2) are at **2300 mm** | DC-S1-1 p1; SN sheets p1 | Headroom under a hard hanging object in crowded areas; directional and station-name signs would hang at different levels in the same space | Is 2100 mm intentional? Which headroom figure has Baku Metro adopted? Recommendation: one overhead level for all ceiling signs unless a site constraint is recorded. |
| C3 | DC-S1-1 | Sign is **1-sided** but ceiling-hung in two-way circulation; the back face is not defined | DC-S1-1 p1 | People walking the other way see a blank back (or nothing) at the same decision point. | C4 | DC-S1-1, SN-C-L-2, SN-S-XL-2 | Overhead fixing: one 10×120 anchor per tube; no anchor type, load check or **secondary safety cable**. | C5 | All types | No fire classification for acrylic, polycarbonate, PVC, vinyl, silicone or lacquer, all used underground | All spec tables; ID-W p2 | Fire load and smoke in an underground station, across hundreds of signs | Provide the reaction-to-fire class for each material, and confirm which fire standard Baku Metro has adopted. |

## C. What these drawings cannot tell us
Placement, sightlines, decision-point coverage and journey continuity can't be judged without station plans, sign location plans, a sign inventory, photos from each decision point and the Baku Metro design manual. See `.claude/skills/metro-wayfinding-audit/templates/information-request.md`.
