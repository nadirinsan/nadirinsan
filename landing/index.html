FAQ

Questions from event teams

Which screens does it work with?

Anything your laptop can connect to: confidence monitors, TVs, projectors and LED walls, over HDMI or USB-C. Move the window to that screen and press F.

Do we need the venue Wi-Fi?

No. The full version runs from a file on the laptop, so it keeps working when the venue network is busy or down.

Can we use it in several rooms?

Yes. Copy the file to the laptop in each room. Each one runs its own countdown.

Will the speaker see the buttons?

No. Once the timer starts, the controls and mouse pointer hide after 3 seconds. Move the mouse to bring them back.

What happens after the free 5 minutes?

The timer stops and offers lifetime access for $49. Your logo and colours stay as you set them.

How do we get the full version?

Right after payment you get an email with a license key and the full timer file. Enter the key in the web version to remove the limit, or open the file on any computer, even offline.

Do I need an account or password?

No. The trial only asks for your name and email. After buying, your license key is all you need on any computer.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $49 once. There are no monthly or per-event fees.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook, in a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Nothing gets installed.
