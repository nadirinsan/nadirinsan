Questions

Before you buy

Do I need internet at the venue?

No. Everything runs from the file on your computer. Without internet it uses your computer's built-in fonts instead of the designed ones; everything else works the same.

Which computers does it work on?

Any Windows, Mac, Linux or Chromebook computer with a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Nothing gets installed.

Can I show it on a second screen or projector?

Yes. Drag the browser window to the monitor facing the speaker and press F for full screen.

Where does my logo go when I upload it?

Nowhere. It stays in the browser on that computer and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Will the beep play?

Yes, once you start the timer with a click or key press. Browsers block sound until you interact with the page.

How do I get the branded edition?

Buy it and reply to the receipt email with your logo file and brand colours. You get your custom file back within two working days.
