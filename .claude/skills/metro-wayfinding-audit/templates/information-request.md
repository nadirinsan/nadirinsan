# Information Request — Complete Station Wayfinding Audit

Sign fabrication drawings alone support a **document-level technical review** of sign types. They cannot show where signs are, what passengers see, or whether journeys work. A complete station audit requires the items below. Mark each Received / Partial / Outstanding.

## A. Station architecture & circulation (essential)
1. Scaled floor plans of every public level (street/entrances, concourse, fare line, intermediate levels, platforms) — DWG or vector PDF with scale.
2. Sections through entrances, vertical circulation and platforms (ceiling heights, beams, suspended-ceiling voids).
3. Reflected ceiling plans (lighting, services, CCTV, PIDs, downstand beams).
4. Vertical circulation schedule: escalators (direction of running), stairs, lifts (levels served).
5. Exit list with numbers and street/destination names; entrance opening hours; step-free status per entrance.
6. Transfer routes (for interchange stations) with the other line's plans.
7. Passenger flow data (peak directions/volumes) or circulation diagrams, if available.

## B. Signage as-built
8. Sign location plans (design and as-built).
9. Signage inventory/schedule: ID, type/code, location, message (all languages), faces, illumination.
10. Photographs: each decision point from every approach direction, each sign face (front and reverse), and context views. Include a scale reference where heights/sizes matter.
11. Message schedule / artwork files for each sign.
12. Drawings for every sign type in the station (incl. **DC-L3-1** directional, safety and map signs) — current revision.

## C. Standards & client requirements
13. Baku Metro wayfinding/design manual (typeface, colours, arrows, pictograms, nomenclature, mounting rules).
14. Adopted legal/contractual instruments: accessibility, fire/life safety, electrical, building and seismic norms; edition and applicability.
15. Emergency signage and evacuation strategy for the station; emergency lighting design.

## D. Technical
16. Material data sheets incl. reaction-to-fire classification (acrylic/polycarbonate, PVC, vinyl, lacquer, paint).
17. LED module/driver data: module variant and count per sign, driver type/location, total load, face luminance and uniformity, TM-21 basis (Lxx, temperature, hours tested).
18. Enclosure IP rating and electrical class; circuit and supply arrangements (normal/emergency).
19. Anchor product, quantity, embedment, substrate, design loads, structural verification; secondary retention for overhead signs; vibration/seismic criteria.
20. Wall cladding/column finish details where signs are fixed.
21. Maintenance procedures: face opening, LED/driver replacement, content updates, cleaning, access equipment, possessions.

## E. Site survey (by or on behalf of the auditor)
22. Measured mounting heights, clearances, viewing distances at key DPs.
23. Ambient illuminance/luminance and glare observations; illuminated sign condition.
24. Accessibility walk-through (wheelchair, visually impaired guidance, tactile provision, help points).
25. Observation at peak and off-peak (crowd obstruction, queues, passenger hesitation points).
26. Advertising positions, sizes and luminance relative to wayfinding.

## F. Budgeting (only if costs are required)
27. Quantities per sign type, unit rates or supplier quotations, access/possession costs, installation constraints.
